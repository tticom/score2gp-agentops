## Goal

REFUSE-15's measurement found 6 of 46 PDFs end without a route record because the CLI refuses before any bar is produced (4 with exit 4, 1 with exit 1, 1 with exit 2). REFUSE-02 and GROUP-01 turned two of the earlier grouping refusals into routes; these six remain. They are real teaching material (maintainer, 2026-11-04). Leads, not facts: re-measure.

## Acceptance

1. Measure first: for each of the PDFs with no route record, the exit code, the exact check that raised it (quote the code path), the page and region it concerns, and what the printed page contains (view it). Counts, codes and located reasons only in the handback. Classify every refused class as a reader gap, a representation gap or unknown, never as a correct refusal: every approved fixture except the blank No Notes file is real teaching material a human can play from (maintainer, 2026-11-04). A bar the evidence cannot yet settle stays refused with a located reason, never guessed, and is reported as unfinished.
2. Pick the largest cause that the page evidence can settle safely and fix the reader or the resolution, not a threshold or a safety check. Design on some of the sources and hold out at least two: apply the finished reader to them once and report bar by bar against the printed notation, without tuning on them. A source the evidence still cannot settle stays refused with a located reason.
3. Report before and after over the whole private set: bars written, bars refused by code, and every source whose written GPIF changed. Every source that converted before must keep a byte-identical inner Content/score.gpif unless a refused bar became written, which the handback lists. Check each newly written bar against its reference where a readable one exists, otherwise against the printed page, and say which. Reference-less sources are labelled not independently verified.
4. A named mutant (drop the new rule) fails a test on a real source; synthetic tests carry no acceptance weight and the handback says so.
5. Nothing that worked may change; validation commands pass; handback complete.

Never edit CI, private fixtures or reference files, agent instruction files or the control plane (`scripts/score2gp_*`, `ORCHESTRATION_STATE.json`, prompts, skills): stop and name the path if one is needed.
