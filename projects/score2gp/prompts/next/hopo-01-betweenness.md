## Goal

The within-bar HOPO pairing ranks note pairs by midpoint distance without requiring the marker to lie between them, so a genuine cross-bar HOPO whose marker is keyed to the destination bar attaches silently to that bar's first pair (review thread N1 on product #467, `src/score2gp/build_ir.py` around the intra-bar loop). Require betweenness, add the backward cross-bar counterpart, and refuse with a located reason when ambiguous.

## Acceptance

1. Reproduce on a real approved source first: find the bars (counts and indices) where today's pairing attaches a marker to a pair it does not lie between, and show the reference `.gp` technique for them.
2. Pairing requires x1 < marker < x2 for within-bar pairs; a marker keyed to the destination bar pairs backward across the barline; an ambiguous marker yields a located refusal of that technique, not a guess.
3. Real-source tests assert the corrected bars and fail under mutants (drop the betweenness test; pair the nearest regardless of side; no backward pairing); every bar whose HOPO pairing is already right keeps a byte-identical GPIF.
4. Validation commands pass; handback complete.
